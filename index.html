About IREducation

Headquartered in Hong Kong and operating across China and the UK through Oxford Advanced Learning, IREducation Ltd. (InteRactive Education) bridges rigorous educational publishing with modern learning technology.

We build unified print and digital learning systems designed specifically for STEM education. Rather than treating software as an afterthought, our textbooks and interactive platforms are developed hand-in-hand—pairing deep subject expertise and accessible explanations with exam-focused practice, real-time assessment, and essential teacher tools.

Our focus is on academically and linguistically specialised curricula that mainstream educational publishers often overlook. Through ethical, purposeful publishing, we work to empower educators, support students, and advance STEM literacy worldwide.

Our Textbooks

Published

Advanced Statistics for Edexcel A-Level — the world's first dedicated textbook for Edexcel A-Level Statistics — was published in June 2026 and is available to buy. Authored by Isaac Chit Hang Teng, published by IREducation Inc., and developed in conjunction with IRMaths, it matches the Edexcel specification with clear explanations, worked examples, and exam-style practice. Designed to be exploratory rather than narrowly exam-focused, it brings together Core, Further and STEP Statistics, with dedicated Beyond the Syllabus sections exploring proofs, historical context, and connections to machine learning, data science, and Bayesian statistics. It is available as a complete book or as separate First and Second Year editions.

Students at more than 10 schools now use the book, and several of those schools have adopted it as a main teaching resource.

Schools can contact us directly for institutional discounts and free shipping, subject to order size and availability.

How We Use AI

Purposeful support around trusted content

AI is integrated into our platforms to help students engage more deeply with curriculum-aligned resources. In IRMaths, it works alongside structured lessons and assessments rather than replacing the textbook, the teacher, or editorial review.

Context-aware tutoring

The IRMaths Tutor can use the lesson, question, or completed assessment a student is viewing to explain concepts, offer hints, check working, and create a similar practice question.

Assessment-safe guidance

During active assessments, the tutor is designed to guide the next step without revealing final answers. After completion, it can use results to support focused remediation.

Adaptive practice and feedback

AI can generate curriculum-grounded practice from selected chapters, while assessment evidence helps identify skills, misconceptions, and areas that need further work.

Insights for teachers

Progress and mastery evidence can be brought together into teacher-facing insights, helping educators see class patterns and direct support where it is most useful.
